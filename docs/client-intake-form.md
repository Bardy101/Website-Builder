# New Website — Client Intake Form
*Master copy. Rebuild this as a Google Form / Tally / Typeform so answers arrive structured. Mark the fields below flagged **[Required]** as required in the form tool — the build slot is only booked once the form is 100% complete.*

> **Why this form matters (put this text at the top of the real form):**
> Everything I need to build your site is in this form. The more complete your answers, the faster and better the result — your delivery timeline starts the day this is fully submitted. Most clients finish it in 20–30 minutes.

---

## Section 1 — The basics
1. Business name (exactly as it should appear on the site) **[Required]**
2. Your name and role **[Required]**
3. Best email + mobile for the project **[Required]**
4. Business address / service area to display **[Required]**
5. Opening hours (or "by appointment") **[Required]**
6. Link to your Google Business Profile, if you have one
7. Current website address, if any

## Section 2 — What you do
8. Describe your business in 2–3 sentences, as you'd say it to a customer **[Required]**
9. Your top 3 services, most profitable first (one line each on what's included) **[Required]**
10. What makes customers choose you over competitors? (years trading, guarantees, accreditations, family-run, response time…) **[Required]**
11. Anything you *don't* do that people often ask about? (helps us filter enquiries)
12. What's the #1 action you want a visitor to take? (call / fill form / book / visit) **[Required]**

## Section 3 — Brand & assets
13. Upload your logo (highest quality you have; a photo of a van/signage works if that's all there is) **[Required — or tick "I don't have one, use styled text"]**
14. Brand colours, if any (or "designer's choice")
15. Upload 5–15 photos: your work, your team, your premises, before/afters. Phone photos are fine — real beats stock. **[Required — or tick "use tasteful stock, I have none"]**
16. Any existing materials worth reusing? (flyers, brochures — upload)

## Section 4 — Proof
17. Paste 2–5 of your best customer reviews (or tell me to pull them from your Google profile) **[Required — Google-profile option counts]**
18. Any accreditations, memberships, insurance or certifications to display? (Gas Safe, NICEIC, trade bodies…) — upload logos if you have them
19. Notable projects/clients you're allowed to mention?

## Section 5 — Practicalities
20. Do you own your domain name (e.g. yourbusiness.co.uk)? **[Required]**
    - Yes → who is it registered with, and do you have the login?
    - No → preferred domain name (I'll help you register it **in your name**)
21. Where should contact-form enquiries be emailed? **[Required]**
22. Do you use any booking/quote system already (Calendly, Fresha, ServiceM8…)?
23. Legal bits: registered company name & number (if ltd), VAT number if applicable — for the footer and privacy page

## Section 6 — Taste (optional but useful)
24. 1–3 websites you like the look of (any industry) — and what you like about them
25. Anything you definitely *don't* want?
26. Tone: pick the closest — Friendly & local / Premium & polished / Straight-talking & practical / Caring & professional

## Section 7 — Sign-off
27. Tick to confirm: **I've read the package scope and understand it includes one consolidated revision round; the delivery timeline starts when this form is complete.** **[Required]**
28. Tick if you'd like: **occasional emails with tips and offers** (optional — unticked by default)
29. Anything else I should know?
