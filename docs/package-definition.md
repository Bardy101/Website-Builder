# Website Packages — Scope & Terms
*Internal master copy. Adapt wording for the public site; prices are suggested starting points — adjust for your niche and confidence. Have a solicitor (or a reviewed template service) sanity-check the terms section before first use; this is a working draft, not legal advice.*

---

## Positioning statement (for your own discipline)

We sell **fixed-scope, fixed-price websites, built once, owned by the client, on a managed platform.** We do not sell ongoing attention. Every clause below exists to protect that.

---

## Package 1 — "Launch" (one-page site)

**Price: £495** · **Delivery: 7 working days from completed intake form**

For businesses with no website, or a single-page presence need.

**Included:**
- One-page website on Framer / Webflow / Squarespace (client's account)
- Sections: hero, services (up to 3), about, testimonials, contact + map, call-to-action
- Copywriting drafted from your intake form and Google Business Profile
- Mobile-responsive, fast-loading, basic on-page SEO (titles, descriptions, headings)
- Contact form or click-to-call setup
- Domain connection (domain registered in **client's** name)
- **One revision round** (one consolidated list of changes)
- 15-minute recorded handover video: how to edit your own text, photos and hours

**Not included:** logo design, photography, copy rewrites after approval, e-commerce, bookings systems, email hosting, ongoing edits.

## Package 2 — "Standard" (up to 5 pages)

**Price: £1,250** · **Delivery: 14 working days from completed intake form**

For established businesses replacing an outdated site.

**Included:** everything in Launch, plus:
- Up to 5 pages (typically Home, Services, About, Reviews/Gallery, Contact)
- Redirects from old site URLs (protects existing Google rankings)
- Google Business Profile link-up and review widget
- Basic analytics installed (privacy-friendly, cookie-banner compliant)
- **One revision round** (one consolidated list of changes)

**Not included:** same exclusions as Launch.

## The terms that protect your evenings

1. **Build starts when the intake form is complete.** Not when the deposit is paid — when every required field and asset is in. Timelines count from that moment.
2. **Payment:** 50% deposit to book the build slot; 50% on approval, before the site goes live on the client's domain. Payment by Stripe link or bank transfer.
3. **One revision round means one consolidated list.** Client gathers all changes into a single reply. Piecemeal changes across multiple messages count against the same round. Further rounds at the add-on rate.
4. **Approval deadline:** if no feedback is received within 14 days of the draft being shared, the draft is deemed approved and the final invoice is issued. (Prevents half-finished projects hanging over you for months.)
5. **Ownership:** the client owns their domain, their platform account, and their content. You retain the right to display the work in your portfolio.
6. **Platform fees** (Framer/Webflow/Squarespace hosting, ~£10–20/month) are paid by the client directly to the platform on the client's own card.
7. **Copy approval is final.** Post-approval rewrites are chargeable edits.
8. **Support boundary:** the package includes the handover video and 30 days of email support for defects (things not working as delivered). It does not include changes, additions, training calls, or support for email, printers, or anything beyond the website itself.
9. **Compliance note:** sites are built with good practice for speed, mobile and accessibility, but no certification of WCAG or GDPR compliance is given; clients remain responsible for their own legal pages and business compliance. (A privacy policy page placeholder is included.)
10. **Cancellation:** deposit is non-refundable once the build has started; before that, refundable minus any completed mockup work at £45/hour.
